Dental Practice KPI Metrics and Benchmarks
=====================================

KPI: Collection %
-----------------
Formula: "total_collections / net_production"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 95 %"
- Target: "99 – 103 %"
- Stretch: "> 105 %"

---

KPI: Case-Acceptance % (by $)
-----------------------------
Formula: "accepted_tx$ / proposed_tx$"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 50 %"
- Target: "60 %"
- Stretch: "≥ 70 %"

---

KPI: Production $/Hr — Doctor
-----------------------------
Formula: "net_production / clinical_hrs"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 300"
- Target: "300 – 500"
- Stretch: "> 550"

---

KPI: Production $/Hr — Hygiene
------------------------------
Formula: "net_production / clinical_hrs"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 120"
- Target: "150 – 200"
- Stretch: "> 225"

---

KPI: Call-Answer Rate
---------------------
Formula: "answered_calls / total_calls"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 80 %"
- Target: "85 – 90 %"
- Stretch: "> 90 %"

---

KPI: Hygiene Re-appt %
----------------------
Formula: "next_hyg_appt / new_patients"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "< 70 %"
- Target: "80 %"
- Stretch: "≥ 85 %"

---

KPI: Write-offs %
-----------------
Formula: "write_offs / (production + adjustments)"

Benchmarks:
- Low: "> 5 %"
- Target: "< 3 %"
- Stretch: "< 2 %"

---
